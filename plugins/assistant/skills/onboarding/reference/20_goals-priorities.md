# Module 20 — Goals & Priorities

Writes `me/goals.md`. Arguably the single most-read file in the whole
system afterward — most sessions should open by consulting it.

## Topics to cover

- **Life/work pillars** — don't presuppose a count. Ask "what are the few
  things everything else you do should be in service of?" and let the
  number fall out naturally (most people land between 3 and 6). Ladder
  each one: "why does that matter to you?" until something concrete and
  non-generic surfaces, not a restated slogan.
- **Live deadlines** — anything with a real date attached, right now.
  Capture the actual date, not "soon."
- **Current top priorities** — distinct from the pillars: what's actually
  getting attention this week or this month, which may be a small subset
  of the full pillar list.
- **Standing goals** — recurring practices, not one-off deadlines (a
  review cadence, a habit, a target that renews).
- **Review cadence** — how often should this file get revisited? A
  reasonable default to offer: quarterly, or at every natural project
  boundary.
- **Opt-in learning ritual** — do they want a recurring "teach me one
  thing" session (feeds the `daily-learning` skill)? If yes, on what
  subject area, and roughly how often (daily, weekly, "whenever I ask" —
  the `daily-learning` skill needs both the subject and the cadence, not
  just the subject)? If no, that's a complete answer — don't install the
  habit by default.
- **Session-start protocol** — ask directly: "should Claude read this file
  and flag anything urgent at the start of a session, or would that get
  old fast for you?" People differ on this; don't assume the answer.

## Interview notes

This is a laddering-heavy module. A pillar stated as "grow my career" is
thin; two or three "why does that matter" iterations usually gets to
something concrete and personal worth actually writing down.

## What gets written

Fill `me/goals.md` completely, including the "How to use this file"
section with whatever session-start behavior they actually want (or
"don't proactively surface anything" if that's their preference), and the
"Learning ritual" section with the subject + cadence, or "opted out" if
they said no.
This is the section `daily-learning`'s own SKILL.md reads to know what to
teach and how often.
