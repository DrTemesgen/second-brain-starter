# Module 50 — Work & Project Domains

Writes `me/projects.md`, and seeds the topic list Module 90 (Folder &
File Organization) will turn into destination routing. Also decides whether
Module 999 includes a "Funding & partnerships" section on the Dashboard.

## Topics to cover

- **What fields/projects/employers/ventures does the user actually operate
  across?** Let them name their own categories rather than offering a
  preset list — could be one employer and nothing else, could be five
  different things.
- **IP/ownership boundaries** — is there a real line between, say,
  employer-owned work and their own work, or between different ventures'
  ownership? This matters more than it seems to at setup time; ask
  directly rather than assuming it's obvious.
- **Where do project files currently live?** Doesn't need exact paths yet
  (Module 90 does the actual routing table) — just enough to know the
  rough shape: "everything's in one folder," "scattered across drives,"
  "cloud-only," whatever's true.
- **Is grant-seeking, fundraising, or investment pursuit a regular part of
  their work?** This single answer gates whether the Dashboard ships with
  a "Funding & partnerships" section at all — ask it plainly rather than
  defaulting the section in or out.
- **Recurring work types** — pay attention here specifically for Module 80
  (Agent Roster): what kinds of tasks come up again and again? This is the
  raw material that module will turn into an actual agent roster.

## What gets written

Fill `me/projects.md` — one category-table section per real category they
named, plus the "Conventions they use" closing section (naming conventions,
draft-vs-final locations, IP boundaries stated plainly).
