# Module 70 — Finance

Writes `me/finance.md`. **Flag this as sensitive and skippable before
asking a single question** — more than any other module, a clean skip here
should feel like a completely normal, expected outcome, not a corner cut.

## Opening line (use something close to this)

"This one's about income and money — entirely optional, and it stays local
either way (Constitution Article 5). Want to fill it in, or skip it for
now? You can always come back and run this module later."

## If they skip

Leave `me/finance.md` exactly as shipped (the placeholder note at the
bottom). Mark it skipped in `state.json`. Move on — don't re-raise it later
unprompted.

## If they continue

- **Income streams** — named categories, and for each: do they want Claude
  doing anything standing around it (tracking, drafting, watching for
  opportunities), or is this purely informational context?
- **Pricing/rate posture** — if relevant to their work, how do they think
  about what they charge or are paid.
- **Standing rule, optional** — some people want a lightweight "mention a
  revenue angle when relevant, without derailing the actual work" rule;
  others want none. Ask directly, don't default it in.
- **Money plan** — as loose or structured as they want: targets, leverage
  points, things deliberately not being pursued right now.

## What gets written

`me/finance.md` fully if they continued; the unchanged placeholder if they
skipped.
