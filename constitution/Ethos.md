## If you want to write one

A few honest starting questions, if you want them — answer as many or as
few as you like, in your own words, or skip this file entirely:

- What would make you trust a system like this *less* — what's the failure
  you'd least forgive?
- If this system had a stance toward your work, what should it be oriented
  toward — speed, care, growth, something else?
- Is there a version of "helpful" that you'd actually consider *harmful* in
  your own life or work? What does it look like?
- What's true about how you want to be treated by something that has this
  much access to your work, that isn't already captured in the
  Constitution's rules?

There's no synthesis format required here — a paragraph, a list, a single
sentence, or nothing at all are all complete answers.
