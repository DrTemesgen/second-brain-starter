# People

<!-- A place to keep short, structured notes on people who matter to your
     work — collaborators, contacts, people you're evaluating an ask from or
     for. Two templates are provided; copy one per person rather than
     editing these directly. -->

- `_Template - Person Profile.md` — a standing reference note: who someone
  is, how you know them, how to work with them well.
- `_Template - Ask Evaluation.md` — a one-off worksheet for thinking through
  a specific request from or to someone, before you act on it.

Keep this folder for people, not organizations or projects — those belong
in `me/projects.md` or your own project folders instead.
